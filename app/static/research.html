How to interpret this study

  • Outages are simulated by replacing every value in a feature group with missing values; they are not captured from a live network.
  • Both review policies receive the same total review count in each scenario. Their category-level review counts can differ.
  • Attack labels are used only after selection to score the review queues; they are excluded from model inputs.
  • Sign tests compare paired lifts across training seeds. Adjusted q-values use Benjamini–Hochberg across the reported scenario and category comparisons.
  • This is a research evaluation on one dataset split, not evidence of production security performance.
